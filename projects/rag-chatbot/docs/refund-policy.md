# Refund Policy

Physical products may be returned within 30 days of delivery with a valid
receipt for a full refund. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

Digital products (downloads, subscriptions, license keys) are non-refundable
once delivered, except where required by law.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5–10 business days
to appear, depending on your bank.

For policy exceptions, contact support@example.com with your order number.
